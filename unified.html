Genami, one design language

Right now the homepage is a dark sage editorial site and the console is a light acid-yellow product. Three directions below, each one applied to both surfaces so you can judge the system, not the screen. Same content in every mock: the hero line, a course row, the sidebar, the overview header and your stat chips.

Direction A

Acid on Ink

The homepage wins. Both surfaces live on the dark base it already has, and the console's acid yellow becomes the single system accent: active states, highlights, progress. Red is demoted to tiny signals only. The product feels like one serious console you log into, and the marketing page is its front door.

Base #0C1210 Surface #141C19 Accent #E8F94F Signal #FF4612 Text #EEF3F1
Homepage: barely changes. Gains acid highlights on key words and buttons instead of coral.
Console: flips dark. Cards, sidebar and topbar become surfaces on ink; acid marks what's active.
Type: Instrument Serif display + DM Sans everywhere. Space Grotesk and Gabarito retire.
Vibe: premium tool, night shift, "professional console for AI skills".

Direction B

Paper & Ink

The console wins. The homepage moves into the console's paper world: warm light grey, white cards, ink text, acid as the marker color and red kept for one hot element per view. Cheapest direction to ship, because the console already looks like this; the marketing page is the only real rebuild.

Base #F1F2EE Card #FFFFFF Ink #111214 Marker #E8F94F Hot #FF4612
Homepage: full rebuild to light. Hero headline gets an acid marker highlight instead of a dark backdrop.
Console: stays as is, tightened. It becomes the reference, not the exception.
Type: Instrument Serif + Space Grotesk (actually loaded, which fixes the current silent fallback).
Vibe: bright classroom, friendly, day shift, Duolingo-adjacent energy without the toys.

Direction C

Quiet Editorial

Neither wins; we pick new, calmer clothes. Cream paper, deep forest green, serif display, exactly one accent. No acid, no red, no neon. This is the Masterclass direction: slower, more expensive-feeling, aimed at the 35-plus business owner who finds acid yellow toy-like. Boldest brand move, most rebuild effort.

Paper #F7F6F1 Forest #1E4438 Ink #17251F Sage #B8CCAE Tint #EBEFE6
Homepage: keeps its editorial soul but trades dark neon for cream and forest; closest to today's mood.
Console: rebuild around white cards on paper with a forest sidebar; calmest learning environment.
Type: Instrument Serif + Hanken Grotesk, wider line height, more air per element.
Vibe: quiet authority, "the serious MBA of AI skills", ages well.
